# JV012 - Rio Vista Specific Plan

September 5, 2024 City Council. Machine transcript available, but the Swagit chapter is a long public-hearings block rather than a narrow item-only clip.

Rio Vista is the strongest approval-path proof point. The transcript describes a specific-plan and General Plan alignment process, development agreement, public benefits, CFDs, infrastructure commitments, EIR, significant impacts, statement of overriding considerations, mitigation monitoring program, and findings of fact.

The record also shows how business-park/light-industrial components were framed as revenue engines for major infrastructure and broader master-plan benefits, not as stand-alone IOS optionality. Staff explicitly distinguished business park/light industrial from warehouse/logistics in the transcript.

Underwriting read: approvals are possible, but the path is expensive, political, and plan-level. This is not support for quick IOS conversion; it is evidence that the city requires a broad public-benefit and infrastructure case for major industrial capacity.
