# JV008 - Truck-Intensive Use Initiation

January 21, 2021 City Council. Machine transcript available.

This is the origin point for the 2021 truck-intensive controls. Council initiated zoning-code, zoning-map, General Plan, and land-use-map work to identify appropriate locations for truck-intensive industrial uses and to protect neighboring residential neighborhoods from excessive truck traffic.

Underwriting read: any parcel thesis based on pre-2021 permissiveness should be discounted. The city moved from reactive case-by-case friction into a formal policy project and moratorium posture.
