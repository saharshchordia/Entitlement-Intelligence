September 21, 2023 City Council. Machine transcript available.

This case supports the distinction between master-plan approvals and simple IOS conversion. The approval path turns on specific-plan-level planning, environmental review, infrastructure, phasing, and land-use framework creation rather than a small-site CUP.

Underwriting read: helpful as a "large projects can get done" proof point, but weak support for an ordinary landowner claiming IOS upside on a light-industrial parcel.
