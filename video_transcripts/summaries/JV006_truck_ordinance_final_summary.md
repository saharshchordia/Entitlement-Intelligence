# JV006 - Truck-Intensive Use Ordinance Final

December 2, 2021 City Council. Machine transcript available; review against source video before direct quotation.

This is the core policy record. Staff described community and Council concerns around truck traffic, residential impacts, blight, infrastructure deterioration, noise, air pollution, congestion, pedestrian safety, and code-enforcement problems. The ordinance established M-H as the location where truck-intensive uses could be allowed, with CUP, and removed truck-intensive uses from M-SC, M-M, and I-P.

The transcript also confirms that the city understood this as a deliberate land-use policy shift. It extended moratorium coverage for selected areas while land-use amendments were processed.

Underwriting read: light-industrial IOS optionality is weak in Jurupa Valley. The first underwriting question is not "can we file a CUP?" but "does the General Plan/zoning already support this without a legislative change?"
