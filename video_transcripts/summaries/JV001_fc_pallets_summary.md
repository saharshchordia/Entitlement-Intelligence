# JV001 - FC Pallets

July 17, 2025 City Council. Machine transcript available; review against source video before direct quotation.

FC Pallets is the strongest IOS-like denial precedent in the current dataset. The applicant sought to legalize and expand a pallet-yard operation through a General Plan Amendment, M-H rezoning, CUP, variance, and SDP. Staff and Planning Commission recommended denial, and Council denied the package 4-0.

Council's concerns were not simply about whether M-H could allow the use. The decision record focused on fire risk, code-enforcement history, unpermitted expansion, residential proximity, right-of-way impacts, site conditions, inability to make findings, and lack of confidence in the operator's compliance posture.

Underwriting read: do not price nonconforming or informal IOS operations as if legalization is straightforward. Capital burden includes cleanup, site hardening, fire access/hydrants, stack-height and separation compliance, ROW controls, utility permits, CUP findings, variance risk, and applicant credibility work.
