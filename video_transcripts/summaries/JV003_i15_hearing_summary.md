# JV003 - I-15 RV / Self-Storage Hearing

February 5, 2026 City Council. Machine transcript available; review against source video before direct quotation.

The project sought approval for an 86.8-acre RV and self-storage facility southeast of I-15 and 68th Street. The requested approvals included GPA, change of zone, tentative parcel map, CUP, and SDP. Staff presentation described a 136,000 sf self-storage building, RV parking, a 72.5-acre preservation area, access-road improvements, frontage improvements, fencing, landscaping, a pump house/fire-access solution, and CEQA/MND review.

The transcript is valuable because it shows the scale of work required before Council even reaches the final vote: long access road design, 68th Street improvements, fire-department response to limited secondary access, open-space/conservation handling, storm/flood grading via borrow area, and detailed conditions.

Underwriting read: RV/self-storage is not a clean proxy for easy IOS. Even lower-operational-intensity storage required a full discretionary package and major civil/site design work, then later moved to a denial resolution.
