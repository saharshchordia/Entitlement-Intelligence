Bottom
Line

Jurupa Valley has not treated IOS/truck storage as ordinary light-industrial optionality.

The Council-level pattern is restrictive: truck-intensive uses were narrowed to Manufacturing Heavy (M-H) with a CUP, while truck/trailer storage yards, heavy equipment storage, auto auctions, salvage yards, and contractor storage yards were removed from I-P, M-SC, and M-M.

Approvals exist, but they are not casual: large logistics approvals generally came through EIRs, development agreements, zone changes, specific plans, MMRPs, and statements of overriding considerations.

The closest IOS-like evidence cuts against a blanket premium: FC Pallets was reportedly denied despite seeking M-H rezoning and CUP/SDP/variance relief, and the I-15 RV/self-storage project moved from an approval package to a Council denial resolution.

05

Feasibility Conclusions

IOS / truck storage in light industrial

Weak feasibility. The 2021 ordinance removed truck-intensive uses from I-P, M-SC, and M-M and retained only M-H with CUP. Treat light-industrial IOS claims as speculative unless current code shows an adopted exception.

M-H with CUP

Necessary but not sufficient. FC Pallets shows that a path involving M-H rezoning, CUP, variance, and SDP can still fail when findings, safety, ROW, or compatibility are problematic.

Large logistics

Feasible only through heavy lift. Rubidoux, Aqua Mansa, The District, and Rio Vista point to EIRs, DAs, CZs, specific plans, SOCs, and mitigation, not lightweight approvals.

Fleet / bus dispatch

Emerging but narrow. Leslie Starks' bus dispatch path required Council to consider a text amendment to allow private bus dispatch in M-SC with CUP and standards.

RV / self-storage

Mixed to unfavorable. I-15 RV/self-storage moved to denial despite a full GPA/CZ/TPM/CUP/SDP package. Self-storage should not be used as an IOS proxy without site-specific proof.

EV charging contrast

EVCS remains the cleaner entitlement story: local code provides expedited nondiscretionary building-permit review, while trucking-adjacent uses require legislative/discretionary paths.
