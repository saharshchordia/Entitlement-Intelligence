Accessory rule in force

Current path: section 4.2.61 allows charging stations as accessory structures in districts permitting commercial uses, including M, subject to its siting, height, signage, lighting, and associated utility-structure standards.

Limit: the section does not independently authorize an IOS yard, truck terminal, or charging operation as the principal use. The underlying use classification and ordinary technical permits still apply.

Operative Rules And Approval Sequence

Data-center definition
The moratorium resolution covers a physical room, building, or facility housing infrastructure to build, run, deliver, or transmit applications and services, or store and manage associated data. It lists no power threshold. The definition governs the moratorium; the denied permanent amendment is not current zoning law.
Data-center gate
The Board approved resolution 2026-1109 by 6-1. It pauses acceptance and disposition of listed SLUP, rezoning, land-disturbance, building, business-license, and occupancy applications for covered new or expanded data centers in unincorporated DeKalb. Lawfully operating facilities are excluded.
IOS classification
Each zoning district and applicable overlay has its own use table. In Bouldercrest Tier 4, storage yard is a special-permit row, while truck stop/terminal and transportation-equipment storage/maintenance are listed YES. The planning director's classification determines the relevant row for actual operations. Tier 4 is not a countywide rule.
IOS approval path
If the use is classified as a Tier 4 storage yard, the SLUP proceeds through Planning Commission advisory review and Board decision, followed by applicable development and construction permits. If classified into a listed YES row, no SLUP is shown by that row; development and construction review still applies. Overlay design, screening, and outdoor noise/odor limits remain relevant.
EV accessory path
Section 4.2.61 treats charging stations as accessory structures. It includes a 15-foot street right-of-way setback, height limits, signage and lighting rules, and separate utility-structure standards under section 4.2.56. It does not classify a stand-alone truck-charging yard as a permitted principal use. The adopted requirements are itemized below.

EV charging: adopted section 27-4.2.61

The Board adopted ordinance 2024-0652 on August 27, 2024. These requirements are from the codified ordinance, not draft language or community-council requests.

TopicAdopted treatmentEntitlement read
Eligible districts and statusCharging stations are classified as accessory structures and allowed in districts permitting commercial uses as a principal or accessory use, expressly including M. Stations may occupy front, side, and rear yards. Sections 4.2.61(F)-(H).Supports charging associated with a lawful use; does not by itself classify an independent truck-charging yard or override Tier 4's principal-use table.
Siting and dimensionsAt least 15 feet from all street rights-of-way. Associated accessory buildings/structures must meet the district side-yard setback or 10 feet, whichever is less, and be at least 10 feet from front and rear lot lines. Charging-station structures cannot exceed 10 feet or the principal structure's height, whichever is less. Sections 4.2.61(B)-(C).Equipment and any proposed canopy or other structure require design-specific review under these limits and applicable overlay rules.
Design, lighting, and utility equipmentDesign must satisfy section 27-4.2.2; lighting must satisfy section 27-5.6.1. Associated utility structures must satisfy section 27-4.2.56, including security fencing and/or landscaping to mitigate visual impact. Sections 4.2.61(A), (D), (G).A transformer or similar component is not exempt from separate utility-structure standards merely because it serves chargers.
Parking and signsCharging spaces may count toward minimum parking under section 27-6.1.16. Each station and dedicated space must be signed for EVs actively charging; signs must comply with chapter 21. Sections 4.2.61(E), (I)-(J).Charging stalls are recognized parking spaces, with active-charging designation and sign review.

Section 4.2.61 does not set a charging-power threshold or expressly distinguish passenger vehicles from heavy trucks. It does not establish serving-utility capacity or displace electrical, fire, site-development, or overlay review.
