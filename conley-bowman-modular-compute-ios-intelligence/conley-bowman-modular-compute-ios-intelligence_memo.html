EV Charging

Codified section 27-4.2.61 treats charging stations as accessory structures and allows them in districts that permit commercial uses, including M. It sets a minimum 15-foot street right-of-way setback, front/rear and side-yard conditions, lighting and signage rules, and separate treatment of associated utility structures under section 27-4.2.56. The Tier 4 overlay and lawful principal-use requirement still matter.

No separate fast-lane finding. The adopted charging section does not establish that a truck-charging yard is a permitted principal use, waive a storage-yard SLUP, or exempt the facility from ordinary site and electrical review. Whether charging here is accessory or itself a principal activity is unresolved.
