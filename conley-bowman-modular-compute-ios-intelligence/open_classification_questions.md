# Open classification questions

- What primary-use row, if any, would DeKalb apply to modular compute under the Tier 4 overlay? The data-center moratorium definition does not establish a permanent zoning permission.
- Would the actual IOS program be a `storage yard` requiring a Tier 4 SLUP, a listed truck stop/terminal, transportation-equipment storage, or another category? The answer depends on parking, storage, freight-handling, and operating facts not supplied.
- Is EV charging accessory to an independently permitted principal use, or a principal charging-depot use? Codified section 27-4.2.61 treats stations as accessory structures but does not itself resolve a principal truck-yard use.
- Can the two principal activities occupy one parcel, and would the compute component bring the entire application within the moratorium? No county determination located.
- Does any later Board action change the March 30, 2027 moratorium expiration? The July 7 Board minutes approve item 2026-1109 by 6-1; its attached resolution sets that date.
