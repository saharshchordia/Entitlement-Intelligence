Parcel Context

The City GIS extract dated 15 September 2026 identifies Washoe County APN 164-352-27, approximately 5.63 acres, Industrial Commercial (IC) zoning, Mixed-Employment (ME) master-plan land use, and an Airport Noise Exposure (ANE) overlay. Use at this parcelWhat IC zoning helps or constrains
Data centerIC is a district in which a data center can seek a CUP, but it is not by-right. Reno's active moratorium bars intake of new data-center CUPs through 31 August 2027 or earlier adoption of adequate rules. The ANE overlay is an additional site-design review consideration, not an exemption. City status; use table.
IOS / truckingThe IC use table lists principal outdoor storage as permitted, subject to screening, setbacks and site standards. A truck terminal is a different classified use requiring a CUP; a truck stop/travel plaza has its own criteria, including a ten-acre minimum that this single 5.63-acre parcel does not meet. The exact activity controls, not the label "IOS."
EV chargingCharging equipment accessory to a lawful use has a technical permit path. A stand-alone charging yard, especially with truck staging, has no separately named principal-use row established in the reviewed use table; the City must classify its primary activity. IC zoning alone does not answer that classification. Use table; building permits.

Data Centers

CUP intake paused

The codified data-center use is conditional in IC. Reno says every new data center needs a CUP and will not accept data-center CUP applications during the moratorium.

Scope
Citywide; includes IC.
Path

Industrial Outdoor Storage

Classification-dependent

Principal outdoor storage is listed P in IC with screening and design rules. Truck-terminal operation is listed C, so dispatch, transfer, staging and parking activity can change the review channel.

Scope
District-specific; parcel is IC.
Path
Use classification, applicable site/building permits; CUP if classified truck terminal or another conditional use.
Authority
Development Services; Planning Commission for a CUP.
Source

EV Charging

Accessory path; principal use unclassified

Accessory chargers can be reviewed as part of an otherwise lawful use with electrical, building, fire, access and parking requirements. No stand-alone charging-yard approval was established by the comparable cases found.

Scope
Citywide technical codes; underlying zoning district governs land use.
Path
Accessory installation: site/technical permits. Principal charging and truck staging: City use classification first; CUP if the classified primary use requires one.
Authority
Development Services, Building & Safety, Fire; utility for service.

Operative Rules And Approval Sequence

Data-center definition
Reno's 2024 administrative interpretation, described in the January 2025 staff report as incorporated into code, defines a facility used primarily for storage, management, processing and transmission of digital data, housing computer/network equipment and associated systems. Air handlers, generators, cooling, storage and substations may be included. The definition is functional; it states no MW threshold and does not exempt modular buildings. Reno's current status page says all new data centers require CUPs. This local land-use definition is distinct from Nevada's tax-abatement definition.
Data-center controls
Title 18, Table 3-1 and 18.03.306(b)(1) list C in IC, cap dock doors at three per building, and govern loading/staging near residential uses. CUP findings and conditions can address operating hours, screening, sound and public services. The 1 June 2026 moratorium prevents intake until 31 August 2027 or earlier adequate regulation; April 2026 text-amendment work is not yet an adopted replacement.
IOS distinction
Table 3-1 and 18.03.306(b)(4) distinguish outdoor storage from truck-terminal and truck-stop uses. Storage screening applies toward roads, parks and nearby residential zoning, with landscaping/fence and material-height limits. IC's P storage row is not permission to run a terminal or a ten-acre-minimum truck stop. Separate review may arise from adjacency, grading, access, lighting and stormwater facts.
EV charging code
The reviewed Title 18 use table does not separately name a principal EV charging use. In the 2025 Title 14 hearing, staff discussed charger-installation guidance and EV accessible parking; that discussion does not resolve principal-use classification. The City says 2024 building codes/2023 NEC govern current plans. Charger equipment needs applicable electrical/building/fire and accessibility review; a truck queue or parking business may trigger a separate land-use category.
Utilities and impacts
NV Energy service, TMWA water, sewer, stormwater, fire, traffic and noise review are fact-specific. Applicants in past hearings described their own will-serve letters, but no site-specific NV Energy capacity, upgrade cost or utility feedback for 3705 Barron Way is established here. A utility letter is not a zoning approval. The ANE overlay and any airport-related standards require parcel-specific plan review. No customer, load, charger count or operating pattern is assumed.
